# App shell (all tabs)

Scope: the whole Flutter app (Home, Map, Report, Guide, Settings, About, startup gate). Mode: Operate. Audience and task per PRODUCT.md: a frightened resident, one hand, often at night, finds the level and SOS in two seconds.

Decided with the user (2026-11-17): direction "Public notice" (picked from three mockups), light and dark themes following the phone, 4 tabs (Home, Map, Report, Guide) plus Settings for language and About; Sensors folds into Home.

Unresolved: real alert/sensor data, report delete/share, sensor history.

## Direction contract

THESIS: The app is a posted public notice: framed blocks set in heavy Thai type, ruled into a board. It refuses the category default of soft rounded cards, pastel banners and a round red hero button.

OWN-WORLD: Light: newsprint paper ground, ink-black 2 px frames and rules, square corners, solid tier fields (green, signal yellow, orange, red). Dark: the same board at night, charcoal ground with paper-coloured frames. Sarabun 700 for headings and values, 400 for body. Selection is an ink block with paper text.

STORY: The visitor reads the level, its advice and its source, sees how to call or where to go, and holds SOS when they need help.

FIRST VIEWPORT: Home. A bulletin strip (area, time) over the framed level block; under it the 4-step level meter; then a row of framed call tiles and the nearest-shelter tile; readings and official notices below. The SOS is a full-width framed red block pinned above the tab bar.

FORM: Public notice board, user-pinned (option B of three); signature move is the 4-step segmented level meter, reused for every gauge. seed: none (unavailable)

FINISH: unreviewed and undocumented is unfinished; this build ends with the finish review, the verdict, DESIGN.md, and every shipping raster carrying its provenance
