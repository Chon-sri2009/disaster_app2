# Bangkok flood persona audit, 100 simulated residents (2026-11-19)

> **These are simulated personas that reason from the app's code and golden screenshots. They are not user research and not interviews.** Every comment is tied to a finding with a code citation below. The simulation finds where the app is likely to fail under panic; it does not measure how many real people would fail. Scenario: flood warning, water arrives in 24 hours, lasts 1 week. Nobody was shown the app on a real device. Checked in this audit: the Dart source and 2 golden PNGs (Home 390x844, Home 360x640 at 1.5x). Not seen: the other golden screens, Thai-text wrapping elsewhere, a real run.

## Production audit

**Production audit: 35/100, blocked. For a real Bangkok flood tomorrow the app cannot deliver the 24-hour warning (no deployed backend, no push), and it does not know where the user is until they press SOS or Report. What it does have, an offline Guide, shelter list, honest "No live data", and a clear SOS, is good but is not the thing people need first.**

Scored as "ready for a real Bangkok flood", not as an average of the features that work. Capped under the skill's rule: no rollback or deploy path for a high-impact release, no deployed HTTPS backend.

### Blockers

- **No warning path exists in a release build** (F2, 56 of 100 personas hit it). With no SENSOR/ALERT_SNAPSHOT_URL, level is null: Home says "No live data" and "not connected to official alerts" (sensor_service.dart:71-81, home_screen.dart:311-319, :627-640). CLAUDE.md: no deployed HTTPS backend, no push. The 24-hour heads-up cannot reach anyone.
- **No area until SOS or Report is used** (F1, 4 of 100 personas hit it). getCurrent() is called only from home_screen.dart:60 (SOS) and report_screen.dart:101 (Add location). On a fresh install Home shows "Area not known yet" (home_screen.dart:143-151), the shelter card says "N saved on this phone, your position is not known" with no nearest shelter (:542-556), and area-matched notices cannot work (:194, :671).
- **Home level is the worst sensor anywhere, not near you** (F10, 7 of 100 personas hit it). SensorService.level takes the worst station across ALL stations (sensor_service.dart:71-81) and Home merges it with the official level (home_screen.dart:187-200) under a header that says "Your last known area". Someone in a dry district can see Danger; someone whose soi floods can see Safe. CLAUDE.md rule 2/D15 says sensors do not raise area alerts yet.
- **SOS sheet order, empty SMS recipient, delayed sheet** (F5, 11 of 100 personas hit it). After the hold the sheet needs GPS (up to 10 s, location_service.dart:94-98) then address lookup (up to 5 s, :41) before it opens (home_screen.dart:60-71, :92). The big red call button is 1669 medical (:816-867), not 1784; SMS opens with no recipient (:889). The dialer still needs a second press (calls_screen.dart:104).
- **Danger advice reads like an order** (F11, 10 of 100 personas hit it). Danger says "Leave for a shelter now" (l10n.dart:26-39). CLAUDE.md rule 2 says the app never issues evacuation orders, and for a 25th-floor condo or a wheelchair user "leave now" can be the wrong advice. Wording is the owner's: flag, do not reword.
- **191 unconfirmed** (F14, 2 of 100 personas hit it). police = confirmed: false (emergency_numbers.dart:25-30); release is blocked while a shown number is unconfirmed.
- **Privacy: GPS goes out, no notice** (F13, 2 of 100 personas hit it). Address lookup sends coordinates to Nominatim (location_service.dart:41) and there is no privacy notice (CLAUDE.md release blockers).

### High-value fixes (proposed, outside the roadmap, need the owner's yes before any code)

1. **Ask for location when the app opens** (with a plain reason) and show its age. Fixes F1 and F3. Touches the privacy question (F13).
2. **Make the Home level per area.** Sensors near the user, or label the level "nearest sensor, name and distance" instead of "your area". Fixes F10. Needs an owner decision against D15.
3. **Do not let SOS wait for the address.** Show the call buttons at once and fill the position in when it arrives. Fixes F5 first half.
4. **SOS sheet: make the disaster hotline (1784) a first-class big button beside 1669**, and let the person pick an SMS recipient or save family numbers. F5. Needs the owner's call on the order.
5. **Report: say "not sent" before saving and add "trapped / need medicine / need evacuation" categories, or remove the word "report" until it is sent.** F4.
6. **Shelter details: open/full, pets, ramp, phone.** Needs a data source; do not guess. F6.
7. **Guide: owner and expert to decide a shelter-in-place section for condos and a 7-day plan, pets, infants, people who cannot move.** I describe the gap; the wording is the owner's. F7.
8. **Review the Danger advice line for people who cannot leave.** F11. DRAFT idea for the owner only: advice that separates "can leave" from "cannot leave, stay up high and call 1784". DRAFT, owner to approve.
9. **Family check-in ("I am OK") text and a 7-day supply checklist.** F12.
10. **More languages, at least ask which (owner decision).** F9.

- `lib/screens/`: `home_screen.dart`, `calls_screen.dart`, `report_screen.dart`, `guide_screen.dart`, parts of `map_screen.dart` and `settings_screen.dart`.
- `lib/services/`: `location_service.dart`, `sensor_service.dart`; `lib/data/`: `emergency_numbers.dart`, `guide_data.dart`, `bangkok_shelters.dart` (111 entries, 19 phones); `lib/models/shelter.dart`; `lib/l10n/l10n.dart` (levels and advice); `lib/widgets/sos_hold_button.dart`.
- Goldens read: `test/goldens/home_phone-390x844.png`, `home_360x640_scale1.5.png`.
- Shelter coverage: a script (kept in scratchpad, not in the repo) using rough hand-estimated district centroids against the shelter coordinates. Distances are approximate, only the order of magnitude is meaningful.

### Evidence missing

- No run on a real phone, no screen reader session, no battery measurement, no real GPS timing for F5.
- Not read: `alert_rules.dart`, `alert_service.dart`, `offline_map.dart`, the alert detail and history screens, the other goldens. Findings about those screens are not made here.
- No CI result, no deploy or rollback docs, no backend test run in this audit.
- No real users. A 5-person usability session on a phone with this scenario would be worth more than any number of simulated personas.
