Disaster Alert & Rescue

A Thai-first flood warning app that works offline and never says "Safe" when it does not know.

Flutter app · small Python backend · ESP32 water sensors

The problem

  • Official flood warnings in Thailand arrive late, and often only on screens people are not looking at.
  • When water rises, users are scared, one-handed, on low battery, with weak or no signal.
  • Goal: tell a person about a warning for their location faster than they would otherwise hear it.

Success test: find the alert level and SOS in under two seconds, with no signal.

What the app does

Home

Big alert band for the user's area, place in words, hold-to-SOS, nearest shelter, area-tagged official notices.

Map

Offline Bangkok map, about 110 shelters, community-sensor markers.

Call

One-tap emergency lines. The user always presses the final call or send.

Report

Photo, location and note, saved on the phone only. It says so before saving.

Guide

Offline first-aid and evacuation topics, in the owner's wording.

Thai by default, English switch. Day and night themes. Works at 1.5× text.

Every state is icon + word + colour

✓ Safe ◔ Watch ▲ Warning ✖ Danger

  • Never colour alone. Tap targets at least 48 dp, text at least 16 sp.
  • Offline is a normal state, never red.
  • Stale, offline, faulty or missing data is "no data", never Safe.

How it works

ESP32 sensors→signed POST→Python backend→HTTPS JSON→Phone app
TMD official CAP feed→poller→/alerts snapshot→Phone app
  • Backend: Python standard library only, SQLite, one process.
  • The phone caches every snapshot and always shows how old it is.
  • If the poller dies, the snapshot's own timestamp ages and the app says "may be out of date".

Rules that never bend

Never a false Safe

Only an official alert can raise a level.

Observations, not orders

Sensors say "community sensor, not an official gauge". The app issues no official warning or evacuation order.

Owner owns the words

Safety text is never reworded. New text is marked DRAFT.

Privacy

Keys stay on the server. GPS stays on the phone unless approved.
